Corporate

For companies and event organisers. They see professional packages, formats, and can enquire about booking you for their event.

Resilience

Design approach

One brand, three lighting states

Same logo, same typography, same James. But each pathway has its own colour temperature and feel — so coaching pages feel energetic, corporate pages feel polished, and resilience pages feel warm and human.

Coaching — fresh greens, white, performance data
Corporate — deep navy, warm metallic, structured packages
Resilience — soft neutrals, sage, editorial photography

Each audience feels like the site was built for them. But it's all one site, one domain, one place to maintain.
