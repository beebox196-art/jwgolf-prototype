# J Whitaker Academy — Your First 30 Days
### A simple plan to turn your coaching into a recognised, profitable business

*Prepared for James Whitaker · August 2026*

---

## The Big Idea

You're not "another golf pro." You're the coach who gives golfers **one clear fix** — not twenty swing thoughts. That's your difference, and it's what people will remember, recommend, and rebook you for.

**Your message, everywhere:** *"James doesn't drown golfers in swing thoughts. He finds the one thing that will actually move the score."*

---

## The Funnel (how a stranger becomes a regular)

**See you online → Trust you → Book an assessment → Get one clear win → Move into a pack or membership**

The biggest leak is usually *after* the first lesson — so the follow-up matters more than the first sale.

---

## Your 30-Day Plan

### Week 1 — Get the foundation right
1. **Homepage becomes a "One Clear Fix" landing page** — one headline, one booking button. No clutter.
2. **Create 3 offers only** (keep it simple):
   - Starter assessment
   - 3-lesson improvement pack
   - Monthly check-in membership
3. **Set up your ChatGPT "saved instructions"** — paste in who you are, the One Clear Fix method, your tone, prices, locations. This 20-minute job makes everything else sound like you.

### Week 2 — Email that pays
4. **Build an email reply template** for enquiries and follow-ups. Reply within 60 seconds of an enquiry — speed is the single biggest lever on getting bookings.
5. **Post-lesson follow-up loop** — after every lesson, send: the one clear fix you worked on, the drill, and a testimonial ask. This drives rebooking *and* builds proof.

### Week 3 — Get found locally
6. **Publish 4 local SEO pages/posts** around what Leeds golfers actually search: "golf lessons Leeds", "driver lesson Leeds", "custom fitting Leeds", "beginner golf lessons near me".
7. **Google Business Profile** — photos weekly, one post weekly, and a saved script for asking every happy pupil for a review.

### Week 4 — Build momentum
8. **Collect 10 testimonials** with handicap, problem, fix, and result. These are gold on your site and socials.
9. **Film once, repurpose everywhere** — one filming session, 6–8 clips. Each becomes an Instagram caption, a 30-second reel script, a Facebook/X post, and a blog paragraph. You film; the tool does the admin.
10. **Chase list** — once a week, nudge people who enquired but never booked, or haven't been back in 8 weeks. This is found money.

## What You Do vs What We Automate

**You do:** coach, film a bit of useful content, and approve the message.
**We automate:** email drafts, content drafts, follow-ups, FAQs, testimonial prompts, website copy.

---

## The Money Model (escape time-for-money)

The trap is selling only one-off lessons. The smarter ladder:
- **Starter assessment** → gets them in the door
- **3-lesson pack** → builds momentum
- **Monthly membership** → recurring value (lesson credit + video review + swing check)
- **Online coaching** → for people who can't get to Leeds
- **Premium custom fitting** → for higher-value clients
