We believe that improvement starts with the right coaching. That's why we offer a variety of ways to enhance your game:

## Custom Fitting – Find the Perfect Clubs for Your Game

Having the right equipment is just as important as having the right technique. At J Whitaker Academy, we specialise in custom club fitting, ensuring that your clubs match your swing for maximum performance.

- Trackman-Powered Fitting – Advanced data analysis to match you with the ideal clubs.
- Trusted by the Pros – We have fitted over 50 European Tour professionals.
- Titleist Team Ambassador – Expertise in finding the perfect setup for every golfer.
- All Skill Levels Welcome – Whether you're a beginner or a low-handicap player, properly fitted clubs will improve your game.

If you want to hit longer, straighter, and more consistent shots, a custom fitting session is a must.
