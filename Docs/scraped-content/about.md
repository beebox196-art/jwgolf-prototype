## Golf Tuition from a vastly experienced pro

James has coached at and run 2 PGA Golf Academies in the UK. He first spent time at the world renowned Belfry working with some of the leading coaches in England and spending time with some of the greatest teachers in the world. From here he moved on to set up the PGA Academy at Oulton Hall, also starting the first Nike Golf Academy in Europe.

Since then James has gone on to be Head Professional at the Moor Allerton Golf Club and Howley Hall Golf Club venues.

James uses modern teaching methods to ensure you achieve your golfing goals, whatever they might be.

James Whitaker has written for Today's Golfer and National Club Golfer as well as providing golf tip videos for Golfshake.com
