## 1. CORE MESSAGE (the 5-second takeaway)

**Hero headline:** "Your Golf Game Is One Clear Fix Away."
**Deck under it:** *One fix per session. Not twenty swing thoughts.*

That's the whole pitch. It's a promise, it's differentiated, and it implies the method. Do NOT lead with the surgeries — the story is proof, not the pitch. Players buy a better round; the resilience earns trust once they're already leaning in.

"Resilient Mind. Free Swing." is the **signature**, not the headline — lock it under the logo everywhere. Retire "Performance Through Clarity" and "Coaching Beyond the Swing" from this page; three taglines is zero taglines.

## 2. SECTION ORDER

1. Logo lockup + signature line (small, top)
2. **HERO** — headline + deck (owns the top third)
3. The One Clear Fix Method — 3 steps
4. Services + free-fitting hook
5. Proof bar — credentials, one line, high density
6. The Story — 3 lines max
7. Contact + brand strip (colours/fonts as a discreet footer, not a section)

Hero and Method are the page. Everything below is supporting evidence, set smaller.

## 3. DRAFT COPY

*Positioning statement (sits beside/below hero):*
"James Whitaker is a PGA Professional in Leeds who coaches golfers to play better with less in their head. Every session ends with one clear fix — something you can feel, repeat, and take to the first tee. Progress beats perfection."

*The One Clear Fix Method — 3 steps:*
**01 SEE** — We find what's actually costing you shots. Not a list. The one thing.
**02 FIX** — One change, one feel, one drill. Coached until it's yours, not just explained.
**03 PLAY** — You leave with a fix that survives the course, not just the mat.

*Resilience line (short, understated):*
"In 2009, five brain surgeries and 50/50 odds took James's swing to zero. He rebuilt it one fix at a time. That's not a story — it's the method."

*Services line-up:*
1-2-1 Tuition · Playing Lessons · Group Coaching · Online Coaching · **Custom Fitting — free**
Hook line: **"Custom fitting, free. Because the wrong clubs aren't a swing fault."**

*Proof bar (one dense line, no bullets):*
"Leeds & District Union Head Coach · Former Head Pro, Moor Allerton & Howley Hall · Built two PGA Academies (The Belfry; Europe's first Nike Golf Academy, Oulton Hall) · 50+ European Tour pros fitted · Today's Golfer, National Club Golfer, Golfshake."

## 4. THE MEMORABLE DEVICE

**"One."** Run the numeral as the spine of the page: one headline, one method, one fix, one page. Set 01/02/03 in gold Garamond numerals, oversized. Pair it with a single gold swoosh rule that repeats as the underline beneath every section head — the swing motion becomes the page's punctuation. Close bottom-right with the signature: *Resilient Mind. Free Swing.*

## 5. WHAT TO LEAVE OUT — the discipline

No prices. No testimonial wall (one 8-word quote max, or none). No full CV or dates. No TrackMan/launch-monitor jargon. No medical detail beyond that one line. No multiple taglines. No social icon row — one handle, one number, one URL. No stock golf imagery; the logo is the image.

Rule of thumb: if it doesn't serve the headline promise, it's off the page.
