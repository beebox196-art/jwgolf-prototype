**Prepared by:** Adam Box & Bee  
**Date:** 28 May 2026

## The Problem

Your current website tries to speak to everyone at once. But the person who wants golf lessons, the company organising a golf day, and someone inspired by your story all need different things from your site. Right now, none of them get what they need quickly enough.

## The Solution

We're proposing **one website with three clear pathways** — so every visitor finds exactly what they're looking for within seconds:

### 🏌️ Fix My Golf
For golfers who want to improve. They see your coaching method, your credentials, and can book a lesson immediately.

### 🏢 Host a Golf Day  
For corporate clients and event organisers. They see professional packages, formats, and can enquire about booking you for their event.

### 💛 Hear James's Story
For people inspired by resilience and wellbeing through golf. They see your journey, your speaking work, and can invite you to speak.

## How It Works

When someone lands on your site, they see three clear options — like flags on a scorecard. They tap the one that's relevant to them and land on a page that speaks directly to their needs. No hunting through menus, no generic content, no confusion.

Each pathway has its own look and feel:
- **Coaching pages** feel fresh, energetic, performance-focused
- **Corporate pages** feel polished, professional, structured
- **Story pages** feel warm, human, inspiring

But it's all one site, one brand, one domain — just three ways in.

## Why This Works

1. **Every visitor feels like the site was built for them** — not for "everyone"
2. **Your story becomes an asset, not an afterthought** — it connects all three audiences
3. **Corporate clients discover your resilience story** — which makes their event more compelling
4. **Golfers see your full credentials** — which builds trust and justifies premium pricing
5. **One site, one domain, one place to maintain** — not three websites to keep updated

## Questions You Might Have

All your content is already saved. Nothing gets lost. We can keep your current site live until the new one is ready, then switch over seamlessly.

**What if I want to change things?**
