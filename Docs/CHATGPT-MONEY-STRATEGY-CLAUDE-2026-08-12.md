## PART A — WHAT JAMES DOES WITH CHATGPT (easiest first)

### 1. Reply to enquiries in 60 seconds
Paste the enquiry in, say: *"Reply as James, PGA pro in Leeds. Warm, direct, no waffle. Ask their handicap and biggest frustration, offer two lesson slots, sign off with my number."*
**Speed of reply is the single biggest lever on conversion** — most pros lose bookings to slowness, not price.

### 2. Build a "saved instructions" profile once (load-bearing step)
Paste in: who he is, the One Clear Fix method, tone (plain, confident, no jargon), prices, locations. Everything after that sounds like him. **This 20-minute job makes everything else work** — if skipped, ChatGPT output sounds generic and James concludes the tool doesn't work.

### 3. Post-lesson follow-up template
Same day: *"One clear fix we worked on today was X. Drill: Y. Do it 10 minutes, three times before we next meet."* The pupil gets results AND he's created a reason to rebook. Ask ChatGPT to turn his scrappy notes into that email.

### 4. Chase list (weekly)
Paste in names of people who enquired but never booked, or haven't been back in 8 weeks. Ask for short, non-pushy nudges. **Found money.**

### 5. Local SEO content (weekly)
One blog post a week answering what Leeds golfers actually type: "how to stop slicing", "golf lessons Leeds", "best time to start lessons". Ask for 600 words in his voice with the One Clear Fix framing. Google Business Profile: photos weekly, post weekly, saved script for asking every happy pupil for a review.

### 6. Social without living on social
One filming session a fortnight, phone on a tripod, 6–8 clips. Ask ChatGPT for reel scripts: *"One Clear Fix — 30 seconds, one problem, one cause, one drill."* It writes captions and repurposes into Instagram, Facebook, X and a blog paragraph. He films; the tool does the admin.

### 7. Money work
Use it as a thinking partner on pricing and packages — but **he sets prices, not the machine.**

## PART B — THE STRATEGY

### Positioning
"One Clear Fix" is genuinely good because it's a **promise, not a slogan** — it says *you will leave with one thing, not twenty*. Own it by making it a format he repeats until it's boring to him: every reel, every blog, every lesson summary literally headed "Your one clear fix." Repetition is what makes a phrase yours.

The resilience story is the emotional hook, but use it sparingly — once on the site, occasionally in longer content, and always pointed at the pupil: *"I rebuilt my swing from nothing."*

### Sequencing (from Claude's follow-up)
1. **Block-booking launch goes to existing pupils BEFORE anything public-facing.** It's the fastest revenue, needs no website traffic, and gives James an early win that makes him trust the rest of the plan. If he starts with content/SEO he'll see nothing for eight weeks and lose interest.
2. Saved-instructions profile is the load-bearing step — someone should sit with him for the 20 minutes.

### Open question for inbox visibility
Whether enquiries come via web form, direct email, phone, or club reception. The reply-speed fix looks different in each case.
