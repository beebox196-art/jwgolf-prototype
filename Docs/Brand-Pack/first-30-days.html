Your First 30 Days

A simple plan to turn your coaching into a recognised, profitable business.

The Big Idea
You're not "another golf pro." You're the coach who gives golfers one clear fix — not twenty swing thoughts. That's what people will remember, recommend, and rebook you for.
The Funnel

How a stranger becomes a regular

See you online→ Trust you→ Book an assessment→ Get one clear win→ Pack or membership

The biggest leak is usually after the first lesson — so the follow-up matters more than the first sale.

The Plan

Your 30-Day Plan

Week 1 — Get the foundation right

  1. Homepage becomes a "One Clear Fix" landing page — one headline, one booking button. No clutter.
  2. Create 3 offers only: starter assessment · 3-lesson improvement pack · monthly check-in membership.
  3. Set up your ChatGPT "saved instructions" — who you are, the One Clear Fix method, your tone, prices, locations. This 20-minute job makes everything else sound like you.

Week 2 — Email that pays

  1. Build an email reply template for enquiries and follow-ups. Reply within 60 seconds of an enquiry — speed is the single biggest lever on bookings.
  2. Post-lesson follow-up loop — after every lesson send: the one clear fix, the drill, and a testimonial ask. Drives rebooking and builds proof.

Week 3 — Get found locally

  1. Publish 4 local SEO pages/posts around what Leeds golfers search: "golf lessons Leeds", "driver lesson Leeds", "custom fitting Leeds", "beginner golf lessons near me".
  2. Google Business Profile — photos weekly, one post weekly, and a saved script for asking every happy pupil for a review.

Week 4 — Build momentum

  1. Collect 10 testimonials with handicap, problem, fix, and result — gold on your site and socials.
  2. Film once, repurpose everywhere — one filming session, 6–8 clips. Each becomes a caption, a reel script, a post, and a blog paragraph. You film; the tool does the admin.
  3. Chase list — once a week, nudge people who enquired but never booked, or haven't been back in 8 weeks. Found money.
