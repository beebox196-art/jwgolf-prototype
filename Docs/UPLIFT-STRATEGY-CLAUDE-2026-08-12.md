## Recommendations — ranked by impact

### 1. Proof, not adjectives
The biggest gap between "another golf pro site" and premium is **verifiable specificity**. "Fitted 50+ European Tour pros" beats the entire luxury design system. Build a dense, factual Credentials strip: PGA, Leeds & District Union Head Coach, Moor Allerton, Howley Hall, Belfry, Oulton Hall, Birmingham, Today's Golfer / National Club Golfer / Golfshake. Logos where licensing permits, text where not. Put an abbreviated version **above the fold** — most visitors never reach the About page.

### 2. Booking friction is where enquiries die
A contact form is not a booking flow. Priority order:
- **Real scheduling** (well-styled embed) > form that pre-selects the lesson type clicked > plain form
- Phone + email in the **header on every page**, tap-to-call on mobile
- Each pricing card gets its own CTA carrying context
- **State the venue** — a Leeds pro's most common pre-enquiry question is "where do I actually turn up?" (location, parking, what to bring, what happens in the first hour)

### 3. Sharpen "One Clear Fix" from slogan to method
As a hook it's genuinely good — names a real pain (contradictory advice, 20 swing thoughts), promises relief. It's currently a headline; make it a **described product**. Show the mechanic: **diagnose → one fix → measurable marker → what you practise until next time**. Three named steps on the homepage. Tells the reader exactly what they're buying; makes the price feel like a system, not an hour.

### 4. Resilience story — one page, one placement, understated
What sells is not the surgeries — it's what they produced: a coach who knows that under pressure people can only hold one thought, and who has personally rebuilt a golf swing from zero. **Lead with the coaching consequence, let the biography support it.** Own section on About + at most one short line on the homepage, in his own voice, no aurora, no drama treatment — restraint reads as strength. Don't position him as inspirational; golfers hire competence.

### 5. Segment the offer
Beginners, improvers, low handicappers, and fitting clients want different pages. "Free custom fitting" is a strong lead magnet, currently buried in a price list — give it its own block explaining why it's free and what he measures.

### Keep
Dark/gold direction, typography, positioning line, bento layout — right register. **Trim the effects (aurora, glass, scroll line) by roughly a third** — premium is spare.
